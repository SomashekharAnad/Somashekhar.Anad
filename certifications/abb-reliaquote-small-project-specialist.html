What it demonstrates

Reading drawings and specifications, doing accurate take-offs, and building optimised bills of materials for switchgear, breakers, safety switches, metering, transformers and NEMA-enclosed controls. It also covers coordinating interrupt ratings and protection, applying value engineering, and managing bids, addenda and revisions through to project delivery.

Together with hands-on DCS, PLC and IIoT work, this covers the electrical distribution side of a project, from the quote to commissioning.
